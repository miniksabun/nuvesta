GREEK GOLDEN VISA

Fact: This is a residency program, not citizenship. Greek citizenship requires 7 year of residency along with language exam after 7th year.

Validity: The residency permit is valid for 5 years and is renewable as long as the property ownership is maintained.

Minimum Investment: Minimum of €250,000 for commercial-to-residential conversions and renovations in buidings. Almost all of them are in mainland, in Athens. No minimum area limit.

Family Inclusion: All family members can benefit within this €250,000 property acquisition. The total cost of state taxes, title deed fees, and service charges for the application should not exceed 10% of the property value for whole family. Covers the main investor, spouse, children up to age 21, and the parents of both the applicant and spouse.

Explanation: Sisters and brother cannot be benefited from Golden Visa, if one of them buys a property. But if their parent buys a property and if sister & brother are lower than 21. In this case, Golden Visa will be valid up to 21 years of age for them. Maximum 24 years if they are student.

A property could be acquired on behalf of children greater than 15 years of age. In this case, both parents could be benefited from Golden Visa, as well.

Higher Thresholds: For standard residential purchases, the minimum is €400,000 areas other than regions of central Athens, Thessaloniki, Mykanos and Santorini and islands with a greater population of 3.100; these requires min. €800,000. Minimum area of the property should be 120 m².

Please be informed that due to high limit regulations and local culture, there is no high-rise residential buildings in Greece being developed after 1980. All alternatives is in the buildings with 5 floors maximum.

Rental Guarantee: Maximum 4% per year in selected projects.

Duration: Goes up to 4 years with 4 year renewals.
