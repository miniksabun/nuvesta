GREEK GOLDEN VISA

Residency Investment Program

Fact: This is a residency program, not citizenship. Greek citizenship requires 7 years of residency along with a language exam after the 7th year.

Validity: The residency permit is valid for 5 years and is renewable as long as the property ownership is maintained.

Minimum Investment: Minimum of €250,000 for commercial-to-residential conversions and renovations in buildings. Almost all of them are in mainland, in Athens. No minimum area limit.

Family Inclusion: All family members can benefit within this €250,000 property acquisition. The total cost of state taxes, title deed fees, and service charges for the application should not exceed 10% of the property value for the whole family. Covers the main investor, spouse, children up to age 21, and the parents of both the applicant and spouse.

Explanation: Sisters and brothers cannot benefit from Golden Visa if one of them buys a property. But if their parent buys a property and the siblings are younger than 21, Golden Visa will be valid up to age 21 (maximum 24 if they are students). A property could be acquired on behalf of children greater than 15 years of age — in this case, both parents could also benefit from Golden Visa.

Higher Thresholds: For standard residential purchases, the minimum is €400,000 in areas other than regions of central Athens, Thessaloniki, Mykonos, and Santorini, and islands with a population greater than 3,100 — these require a minimum of €800,000. Minimum area of the property should be 120 m².

Please be informed that due to high-limit regulations and local culture, there are no high-rise residential buildings in Greece developed after 1980. All alternatives are in buildings with 5 floors maximum.

Rental Guarantee: Maximum 4% per year in selected projects.

Duration: Valid for 5 years with 5-year renewals.
